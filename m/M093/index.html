PVB is polyvinyl butyral. Makers sell it for investment-casting patterns and decorative pieces, and makers stress that alcohol smooths its surface. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 67 °C, just below its glass transition. On the H2C it prints at 200⁠–⁠220 °C without chamber heat, but makers call for keeping it dry.

Partly documented Tensile strength (Z) rests on 2 products, notched Charpy impact, HDT at 0.45 MPa and price on 3 products each and tensile modulus (XY) on 4 products. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against PLA, PVB is 2.6 times as expensive and 48 % less tough in notched impact, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 11 °C higher. Against PETG, PVB is 3.3 times as expensive, 43 % less tough in notched impact and 30 % stiffer. Among the materials, PVB has a low notched Charpy impact, HDT at 0.45 MPa and density: each is in the lowest quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PVB typically reaches a tensile strength of 50 MPa at a modulus of 2.21 GPa, and breaks at 9.85 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 72 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.22 GPa.

What makers say about printing it

Printing advice

  • Increase printing temperature to 220˚C if printing at this fast 15mm^3/s. Polymaker Polycast, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Keep fan speed on at full blast for best surface quality. Polymaker Polycast, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • The burnout temperature for our PolyCast is 1,100 - 1,200˚C to entirely burnout. Polymaker Polycast, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

Moisture sensitivity

  • It is highly recommended to use the PolyBox™ when printing with PolySmooth™ and to store it in the resealable bag. Polymaker PolySmooth, data sheet, p. 4
  • It is highly recommended to use the PolyBox™ when printing with PolyCast™ and to store it in the resealable bag. Polymaker Polycast, data sheet, p. 4

On PVB in general

  • Highly moisture-sensitive filament; keep dry. Moisture after Prusa Research, PVB | Prusa Knowledge Base

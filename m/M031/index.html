ASA is acrylonitrile styrene acrylate. Makers sell it for outdoor and weather-exposed parts, citing its UV resistance. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 96 °C, below its glass transition, and across the layers a part keeps about 59 % of its flat tensile strength. On the H2C it prints with a standard nozzle, but Bambu Lab's Filament Guide requires an enclosure, and some products ask for more chamber heat than the H2C reaches.

Among the materials

Against ASA-CF, ASA is 2.1 times as tough in notched impact, 46 % less stiff and 39 % less expensive. Against ASA-GF, ASA is 9 % less stiff. Among the materials, ASA has a low density and price: both are in the lowest quarter of the materials that publish them. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), ASA typically reaches a tensile strength of 38.6 MPa at a modulus of 2.1 GPa, and breaks at 6.7 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 64.2 MPa at a flexural modulus of 1.97 GPa.

Thermal properties

ASA's HDT is typically 96 °C under 0.45 MPa and 86 °C under 1.8 MPa, with its glass transition typically at 103 °C. As published, Vicat softening is typically 96 °C.

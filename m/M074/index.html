PPS-GF is glass-fibre-reinforced polyphenylene sulfide, a semicrystalline polymer that a maker sells for automotive, aerospace, chemical-industry and electrical parts. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 236 °C, a figure measured after annealing. On the H2C its sheets ask for a hardened nozzle and for drying the filament before it prints.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PPS-GF typically reaches a tensile strength of 72 MPa at a modulus of 5.76 GPa, and breaks at 1.6 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 112 MPa at a flexural modulus of 4.53 GPa.

Thermal properties

Its products put HDT at 236 °C under 0.45 MPa and 126 °C under 1.8 MPa, against a glass transition of 95 °C. Semicrystalline, it carries light load well above its glass transition. As published, Vicat softening is typically 265 °C and melting typically 279 °C.

What makers say about printing it

Warping and shrinkage

  • Shrinkage Rate* Specially-defined 0.21% - 0.42% IPCON PPS-GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Fiberon™ PPS-GF20 is a 20% glass fiber-reinforced polyphenylene sulfide engineered to deliver enhanced mechanical strength and dimensional stability. Polymaker Fiberon PPS-GF20, data sheet, p. 1

Nozzle wear

  • Compatible Nozzle Material Hardened steel, diamond, tungsten carbide, etc IPCON PPS-GF, data sheet, p. 2
  • Abrasion of the brass nozzle happens frequently when printing Fiberon™ PPS-GF20. Polymaker Fiberon PPS-GF20, data sheet, p. 1
  • A wear-resistance nozzle, such as hardened steel and ruby nozzle, is highly recommended to be used with Fiberon™ PPS-GF20. Polymaker Fiberon PPS-GF20, data sheet, p. 1

Moisture sensitivity

  • Fiberon™ PPS-GF20 should always be stored and used under dry conditions (relative humidity below 20%). Polymaker Fiberon PPS-GF20, data sheet, p. 1

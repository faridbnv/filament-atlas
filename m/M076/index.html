BVOH is butenediol vinyl alcohol copolymer, a support material. A maker made it for dual-extruder printers, and resin reference data lists it as water soluble. Its glass transition is 68 °C, and a maker warns that it can soften in warm environments. On the H2C it prints at 190⁠–⁠210 °C without chamber heat, but makers call for drying.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY), tensile strength (Z) and price rest on 1 product each and notched Charpy impact and HDT at 0.45 MPa are not published. Among the materials

Among the materials, BVOH has a high price and a low tensile strength (XY) and tensile strength (Z): each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), BVOH typically reaches a tensile strength of 33.7 MPa at a modulus of 2.34 GPa, and breaks at 15 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 53.8 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.24 GPa.

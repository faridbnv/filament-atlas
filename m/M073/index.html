PPS-CF is carbon-fibre-filled polyphenylene sulfide. A maker sells it for expanded-foam mould tooling and railway parts, and makers say it resists solvents, acids and alkalis. It is semicrystalline, so it carries light load well above its glass transition: its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 255 °C. On the H2C it needs a hardened nozzle and drying at 100⁠–⁠140 °C, 2⁠–⁠12 h, and some products ask for more chamber heat than the printer reaches.

Partly documented Notched Charpy impact rests on 1 product, price on 2 products and tensile strength (Z) and HDT at 0.45 MPa on 3 products each. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PPS-CF typically reaches a tensile strength of 75 MPa at a modulus of 7.73 GPa, and breaks at 1.7 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 113 MPa at a flexural modulus of 6.09 GPa.

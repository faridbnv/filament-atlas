ABS is acrylonitrile butadiene styrene. Makers sell it for functional prototypes, housings, jigs and tooling. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 95 °C, and a part keeps about 73 % of its flat tensile strength across the layers. On the H2C it prints with a standard nozzle, but makers and Bambu Lab's Filament Guide call for an enclosure, and some products ask for more chamber heat than the H2C reaches.

Well documented 89 documents from 32 publishers, 68 of them data sheets. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar. Products with an added filler or additive, and for notched impact those sold as toughened, are shown but kept out of the medians.

Among the materials

Against ABS-CF, ABS is 49 % less stiff, 21 % weaker flat and 11 % less expensive. Against ABS-GF, ABS is 2.9 times as tough in notched impact, 25 % weaker flat and 20 % less stiff. Among the materials, ABS has a high notched Charpy impact and a low tensile strength (XY), density and price: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), ABS typically reaches a tensile strength of 34.9 MPa at a modulus of 2.2 GPa, and breaks at 8.81 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 63 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.07 GPa.

Thermal properties

ABS's HDT is typically 95 °C under 0.45 MPa and 85 °C under 1.8 MPa, with its glass transition typically at 105 °C. One product publishes far more at 0.45 MPa: Polymaker ABS Max (118 °C). As published, Vicat softening is typically 97 °C.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Print complex, ready-to-use parts including end-use, factory tooling, and functional prototypes with demanding geometries. BigRep ABS, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Bambu ABS filament offers exceptional heat resistance (Heat Deflection Temperature: 87 °C) coupled with commendable mechanical properties. This makes it an outstanding choice for everyday functional parts that require high-temperature resistance. Bambu Lab ABS, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • ABSpro™ is extremely suitable for printing mechanical parts with exceptional endurance properties. FormFutura ABSpro, data sheet, p. 1
  • Economical technical housings and parts that benefit from acetone finishing. after Prusa Research, ABS | Prusa Knowledge Base

What makers warn about

  • In terms of other performances, after multi-party comparison tests, the interlayer bonding strength of the new ABS+ has been significantly improved at present, and the Z-axis tensile strength can reach 33MPa, which is at a leading level among ABS materials on the market. In addition, the heat deflection temperature of the new ABS+ can reach 89°C , which is also a certain improvement compared to ordinary ABS materials (around 75°C). eSUN ABS+, maker's product page or guide, p.1
  • However, the disadvantage is that it warps easily and stinks when printing. Bambu Lab ABS, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • Additionally, it can be acetone polished to eliminate layer lines and further enhance the print quality. eSUN eABS HS, data sheet, p. 1
  • Prints produced with ABS Matte have a distinct matte surface finish. Flashforge ABS Matte, data sheet, p. 1
  • With ABSpro™ you can 3D print extremely strong and high gloss objects with a remarkable fine detail. FormFutura ABSpro, data sheet, p. 1
  • With ABS Prime you will create precise, detailed prints that are both durable and have a beautiful surface finish. AzureFilm ABS Prime, data sheet, p. 1

Density and price

ABS's density is typically 1,050 kg/m³, the middle half 1,040⁠–⁠1,060 kg/m³, across 46 products. ABS typically costs 40 CAD/kg, the middle half 31.5⁠–⁠52 CAD/kg, across the 9 products with a regular price in the sample; the lowest is eSUN ABS+ at 25 CAD/kg.

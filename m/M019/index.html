PLA-GF is glass-fibre-filled polylactic acid. Makers sell it for prototypes of mechanical parts that need rigidity and for lightweight parts, and they describe low shrinkage. On the H2C it prints at 215⁠–⁠230 °C with no chamber heat, but some products need a hardened nozzle, and a maker also calls for annealing.

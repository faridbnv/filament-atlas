PLA Silk is a silk-effect PLA with a glossy, satin-like surface. Makers sell it for decorative pieces, jewelry and artistic models. It is amorphous as printed, so it softens at its glass transition: its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 55 °C, and across the layers it keeps 30 % of its flat tensile strength. On the H2C it needs no heated chamber, and makers advise drying before printing.

Among the materials

Against PLA-CF, PLA Silk is 70 % tougher in notched impact, 33 % less stiff and 31 % weaker across the layers. Against PETG, PLA Silk is 88 % tougher in notched impact and 44 % weaker across the layers, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 15 °C lower. Among the materials, PLA Silk has a low tensile strength (Z), HDT at 0.45 MPa and price: each is in the lowest quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PLA Silk typically reaches a tensile strength of 46.3 MPa at a modulus of 1.88 GPa, and breaks at 11 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 75.2 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.46 GPa.

Thermal properties

PLA Silk is amorphous as printed, so it softens at its glass transition, typically 59 °C: its HDT is 55 °C under 0.45 MPa and 50⁠–⁠56 °C under 1.8 MPa. As published, Vicat softening is typically 56 °C and melting typically 164 °C.

Density and price

PLA Silk's density is typically 1,240 kg/m³, the middle half 1,230⁠–⁠1,250 kg/m³, across 32 products. PLA Silk typically costs 31 CAD/kg, the middle half 29.5⁠–⁠38 CAD/kg, across the 6 products with a regular price in the sample; the lowest is eSUN PLA-Silk at 29 CAD/kg.

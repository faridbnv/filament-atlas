PC-ABS is a blend of polycarbonate and ABS. Makers sell it for prototyping, tooling, low-volume production and automotive trim, and say it takes metal plating well. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 112 °C. On the H2C it prints at 260⁠–⁠280 °C, but some sheets ask for a heated chamber beyond what the H2C reaches, and a maker advises a temperature-controlled chamber for large parts.

Partly documented Tensile strength (Z) rests on 2 products, notched Charpy impact and price on 3 products each and tensile modulus (XY) on 4 products. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against PC-CF, PC-ABS is 60 % less stiff and 40 % weaker flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 16 °C lower. Against PC-GF, PC-ABS is 28 % stronger across the layers and 10 % stronger flat. Among the materials, PC-ABS has a high notched Charpy impact and a low tensile modulus (XY): both are in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish them. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PC-ABS typically reaches a tensile strength of 41.8 MPa at a modulus of 1.84 GPa, and breaks at 16 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 70.8 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.3 GPa.

Thermal properties

PC-ABS's HDT is typically 112 °C under 0.45 MPa and 90⁠–⁠108 °C under 1.8 MPa, with its glass transition typically at 109 °C. As published, Vicat softening is typically 113 °C.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • can be used to create secure componants for enclosed spaces. Nanovia PC-ABS Rail, data sheet, p. 1
  • Automotive trim, electronic 3C, toy decoration SUNLU PC/ABS, data sheet, p. 1
  • PC-ABS is suitable for a variety of applications that include prototyping, tooling and low-volume production. Stratasys PC-ABS, data sheet, p. 2

What makers warn about

  • For an outdoor usage, it’s recommended either paint or apply a protective UV coating, both polycabonate and ABS are sensitive to UV radiation, and might “yellow” or crack otherwise in the long term. Nanovia PC-ABS Rail, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • Good combination with metal coating, plastic plating SUNLU PC/ABS, data sheet, p. 1
  • Polymaker™ PC-ABS is a PC/ABS polymer blend which offers excellent toughness and heat resistance while displaying good surface finish and good compatibility with metal plating. Polymaker PC ABS, data sheet, p. 2
  • Flame Retardant you can 3D print extremely strong and high gloss, self-extinguishing objects with a remarkable fine detail. FormFutura ABSpro - Flame Retardant, data sheet, p. 1

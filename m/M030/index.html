ABS-ESD is electrostatic-dissipative acrylonitrile butadiene styrene. Makers sell it for prototypes, end-use parts and protecting electronics, and a maker advises against insulating paints, which would spoil its ESD properties. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 97 °C. On the H2C it prints at 260⁠–⁠265 °C, but most products call for drying, and a maker says it absorbs moisture easily.

Partly documented Tensile strength (Z) and price rest on 2 products each and notched Charpy impact is not published. Among the materials

Against ABS-CF, ABS-ESD is 5.0 times as expensive, 51 % less stiff and 26 % weaker across the layers. Against ABS-GF, ABS-ESD is 29 % weaker across the layers, 25 % weaker flat and 23 % less stiff. Among the materials, ABS-ESD has a high price and a low tensile strength (XY) and density: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), ABS-ESD typically reaches a tensile strength of 35 MPa at a modulus of 2.13 GPa, and breaks at 4.4 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 62 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.31 GPa.

What makers say about printing it

Moisture sensitivity

  • As the ESD ABS filament absorbs moisture easily, it should be dried before being used. Flashforge ESD ABS, data sheet, p. 3
  • Drying Recommendations: 80°C, 2-4 hours 3D4Makers ABSESD, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
More from makers: odour and emissions, printing advice, warping and shrinkage and supports and removal

Odour and emissions

  • Thanks to this advanced production process, the residual amounts of solvents and monomers used in the production process in the final ABS product are extremely low, so the consumables are printed The process has low odor. iSANMATE ESD ABS, data sheet, p. 1
  • Whilst printing ABS produces a VOC derivative of styrene. Nanovia ABS ESD, data sheet, p. 1

Printing advice

  • Easier to print than most industrial-grade materials 3DXTECH 3DXSTAT ESD ABS, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Its functional temperature of up to 100 °C makes it particularly suited for the protection of electronics that can heat up, such as mother boards. Nanovia ABS ESD, data sheet, p. 1
  • It is an ideal filament for applications in automotive, aerospace, semiconductor industry, electronic and electrical industries. Flashforge ESD ABS, data sheet, p. 1
  • it is suitable for areas that req uire electrostatic protection for precision electronic components, integrated circuits and their packages. eSUN ABS-ESD, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • To preserve the fillament’s ESD properties, pieces should not be covered using insulating paints. Nanovia ABS ESD, data sheet, p. 1
  • Smooth Print Surface eSUN ABS-ESD, data sheet, p. 1

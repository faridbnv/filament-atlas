PC FR is flame-retardant polycarbonate. Makers sell it for electrical and electronic parts and for automotive, railway and aerospace uses. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 112 °C. On the H2C its makers recommend an enclosure, and a maker's heated chamber window is above what the H2C's chamber reaches.

Among the materials

Against PC-CF, PC FR is 54 % less stiff and 9 % weaker flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 16 °C lower. Against PC-GF, PC FR is 67 % stronger flat and 63 % stronger across the layers. Among the materials, PC FR has a high tensile strength (XY) and a low elongation at break (XY): both are in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish them. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PC FR typically reaches a tensile strength of 63.5 MPa at a modulus of 2.15 GPa, and breaks at 2.94 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 93.3 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.2 GPa.

What makers warn about

  • Tested to meet the UL 94-2023 V-0 standard, the top level of flame resistance for 3D printing filament, Bambu PC FR is engineered to prevent or slow flame propagation with exceptional self-extinguishing properties. Ideal for critical applications in electronics, automotive, and mechanical engineering. * UL 94 is a plastics flammability standard established by Underwriters Laboratories (USA). V-0—the highest UL 94 V rating—indicates that burning stops within 10 seconds on a vertically positioned specimen, with dripping particles allowed as long as they are not ignited. Bambu Lab PC FR, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

PLA-NF is polylactic acid filled with natural fibre. Makers sell it for rapid prototyping and, for its organic look, decorative objects and toys. Its glass transition is 55 °C. On the H2C a maker says a hardened nozzle is not needed, and makers note that printing temperature changes its colour and its surface.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • The material has been developed for rapid prototyping applications. Extrudr FLAX, data sheet, p. 1
  • The organic look of this filament makes it ideal for decorative objects and toys. Nanovia PLA Flax, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • This biosourced composite 3D filament, certified endocrine disruptor free, can be browned by varying the printing temperature up to 260 °C. Nanovia PLA Flax, data sheet, p. 1
  • At high temperatures and uniform printing speed, the surface becomes rough and resembles sandstone. Extrudr FLAX, data sheet, p. 1

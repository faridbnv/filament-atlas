What the maker says (5)
  • BenefitsLCP are very resistant to weak acids and bases, alcohols, aromatics, chlorinated hydrocarbons, esters, ketones and all chemicals that can cause stress cracks in most other plastics.Fabru, Liquid Crystal Polymer (LCP)p. 1
  • BenefitsThe UV resistance is very good and printed parts made of LCP are therefore also suitable for outdoor use.Fabru, Liquid Crystal Polymer (LCP)p. 1
  • BenefitsLiquid crystal polymer is inherently flame retardant and therefore does not have to be additionally mixed with additives.Fabru, Liquid Crystal Polymer (LCP)p. 1
  • Pitfalls and limitationsParts that are printed from LCP have a strong anisotropy.Fabru, Liquid Crystal Polymer (LCP)p. 1
  • Adhesion between layersThe layer adhesion is not particularly good as a result.Fabru, Liquid Crystal Polymer (LCP)p. 1

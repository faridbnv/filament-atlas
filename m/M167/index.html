TPE, maker-undisclosed elastomer is a thermoplastic elastomer whose makers do not name the elastomer. A maker sells its oyster-shell-based composite for flexible projects in everyday use and calls it compostable. On the H2C it prints at 220⁠–⁠230 °C, and drying is optional, at 50⁠–⁠60 °C for 4 h.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY) and tensile strength (XY) rest on 1 product each and tensile strength (Z), notched Charpy impact and price are not published. Among the materials

Among the materials, TPE, maker-undisclosed elastomer has a high elongation at break (XY) and a low tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY) and density: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), TPE, maker-undisclosed elastomer typically reaches a tensile strength of 18 MPa at a modulus of 150 MPa, and breaks at > 400 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 10 MPa at a flexural modulus of 150 MPa.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Using Nanovia Istroflex, biodegradable oyster shell based composite 3D filament, 3D print every day use flexible projects. Nanovia ISTROFLEX, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

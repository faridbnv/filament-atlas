PA612-CF is a carbon-fibre-filled polyamide 612 nylon. Makers sell it for jigs, fixtures and end-use components in automotive, aerospace and robotics, citing dimensional stability. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 110⁠–⁠175 °C, and across the layers it keeps 50 % of its flat tensile strength. On the H2C it prints without chamber heat, but needs a hardened nozzle and drying.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PA612-CF typically reaches a tensile strength of 77 MPa at a modulus of 4.88 GPa, and breaks at 3.62 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 114 MPa at a flexural modulus of 3.92 GPa.

After printing

Three products publish values measured after annealing, on schedules from 80 °C for 12 h to 100 °C for 8 h.

  • After printing, it is recommended to anneal the model with support structure in the oven at 80-100°C for 8-12 hours. Raise3D Industrial PA12 CF+, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Moisture conditioning refers to allowing the print to absorb moisture. This is inevitble since nylons are hygroscopic, but you can expedite the process by keeping the print in a high humidity climate, or submerging them in water, for 48 hours. All of our test specimens were annealed at 100˚C for 16 hours, and then immerged in water at 60˚C for 48 hours. The average moisture content of specimens is 2.57%. Polymaker Fiberon PA612-CF15, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • After the printing is completed, the printed part can be annealed to further improve the strength of print part. QIDI PA612-CF, data sheet, p. 4

What makers say about printing it

Warping and shrinkage

  • After annealing, max. 0.4%-dimensional shrinkage could be observed in Z-axis depending on infill and layer height, no significant dimensional shrinkage in XY-axis. Raise3D Industrial PA12 CF+, data sheet, p. 3

Moisture sensitivity

  • Dry PA12 CF+ at 80°C for 12 hours before printing, moisture content is crucial for final printed partquality. Raise3D Industrial PA12 CF+, data sheet, p. 3
More from makers: nozzle wear, supports and removal, printing advice and adhesion between layers

Nozzle wear

  • Normally, the life of a brass nozzle would be approximately 9h. Polymaker Fiberon PA612-CF15, data sheet, p. 1

Supports and removal

  • Please remove the support structure after annealing. Raise3D Industrial PA12 CF+, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

Printing advice

  • Besides, it is recommended that the thickness of the heating block is longer 12mm. QIDI PA612-CF, data sheet, p. 4

Adhesion between layers

  • Compared with Raise3D Industrial PA12 CF, it exhibits higher strength-to-weight ratio, better interlayer bonding quality (Z-direction strength) and better dimensional stability. Raise3D Industrial PA12 CF+, data sheet, p. 1

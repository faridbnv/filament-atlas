What the maker says (12)
  • BenefitsQIDI PA612-CF is the one of the lowest density FDM industrial printing material, which can easily meet the high strength and lightweight requirements of industrial applications.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 1
  • BenefitsQIDI PA612-CF has a low coefficient of friction, self-lubricating properties and excellent wear resistance, which can easily meet all kinds of high-strength gears or industrial applications with high wear requirements.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 1
  • BenefitsQIDI PA612-CF is specially developed for FDM 3D printing process, and its substrate material is PA612 long-chain nylon, which has low density, low moisture absorption, high strength, high abrasion resistance, excellent chemical resistance and high heat resistance.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 2
  • Warping and shrinkageSmart Fiber Reinforced Technology greatly improves the mechanical properties and heat resistance of the material, and releases the internal stress during the printing process through the fiber mesh structure, resulting in good dimensional stability of the printed part and no warpage.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 1
  • Warping and shrinkageIt also has good dimensional stability, no warpage and no shrinkage during the printing processQIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 2
  • Moisture sensitivityQIDI PA612-CF based on modified PA612 long-chain nylon, whose saturated moisture absorption rate is only one tenth of ordinary PA6, completely solving the defects of the mechanical properties and dimensional stability of nylon materials that change greatly after absorbed moisture.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 1
  • Moisture sensitivityNylon material is very easy to absorb moisture within the environment, and printing after absorbing moisture will result ozzing, extruding with bubbles and rough surface appearance, thus reducing print quality.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 3
  • Moisture sensitivityIt is recommended that put the filament into a dry box (humidity below 15%) immediately after opening the QIDI PA612-CF vacuum foil bag for printing.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 3
  • Nozzle wearIt is recommended to use hardened steel nozzles, which can effectively improve the print quality.QIDI, Technical Data Sheet QIDI PA612-CFp. 4

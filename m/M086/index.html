OBC is an olefin block copolymer, a polyolefin whose glass transition is -13 °C. Makers sell it for low warpage, chemical resistance, and impact and wear resistance. On the H2C the sheets that state a chamber say none is needed, and its sheets give a bed window of 90⁠–⁠105 °C.

Among the materials

Against PLA, OBC is 74 % weaker flat and 64 % weaker across the layers. Against POM / Acetal, OBC is 79 % weaker flat. Among the materials, OBC has a high elongation at break (XY) and notched Charpy impact, and a low tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY), tensile strength (Z) and density: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), OBC typically reaches a tensile strength of 12 MPa at a modulus of 200 MPa, and breaks at 455 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 9.4 MPa at a flexural modulus of 272 MPa.

TPU 90A class is a flexible thermoplastic polyurethane. Makers sell it for automotive dust covers, bellows, flexible mounts, cable management and prosthetic uses. Printed flat it breaks at 580 % elongation, and across the layers it keeps 81 % of its flat tensile strength. On the H2C makers advise drying it and not annealing it.

Partly documented Tensile strength (Z) and price rest on 2 products each, tensile modulus (XY) on 3 products and notched Charpy impact is not published. Among the materials

Against TPU-CF, TPU 90A class is 60 % less expensive. Against TPU 95A class, TPU 90A class is 45 % weaker across the layers, 40 % less stiff and 39 % more expensive. Among the materials, TPU 90A class has a high elongation at break (XY) and a low tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY) and tensile strength (Z): each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Its density and price sit in the middle half.

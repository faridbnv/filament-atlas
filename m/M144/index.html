PCTG-CF is a carbon-fibre-filled PCTG copolyester. A maker sells it for tooling, equipment and manufacturing aids, wear-resistant parts and end-use parts, and stresses its low shrinkage. On the H2C it prints at 250⁠–⁠270 °C without chamber heat, but makers call for a hardened nozzle, because the carbon fibres are abrasive, and for drying before printing.

What makers say about printing it

Nozzle wear

  • We recommend to use ruby nozzles or hardened steel nozzles. FormFutura AthenaX CF10, data sheet, p. 1

Moisture sensitivity

  • PCTG is moderately hygroscopic and therefore it is recommended to pre-dry the filament at 75°C for approximately 24 hours before usage FormFutura AthenaX CF10, data sheet, p. 1
More from makers: warping and shrinkage and adhesion between layers

Warping and shrinkage

  • The use of carbon fibres increases the thermal resistance of the filament, further reduces shrinkage and gives the characteristic “carbon” matt appearance to the printed models. Spectrum pctg cf10, data sheet, p. 1

Adhesion between layers

  • Its low shrinkage factor and perfect layer adhesion make AthenaX CF10 a breeze to print with. FormFutura AthenaX CF10, data sheet, p. 1

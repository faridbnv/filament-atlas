PEI / ULTEM is polyetherimide. Makers sell it for medical tools that withstand autoclave and gamma sterilisation, for electrostatic-discharge protection, and for aerospace and automotive parts. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 208 °C. On the H2C it is out of reach: most products ask for 370⁠–⁠380 °C at the nozzle, and more bed heat than the printer gives.

Partly documented Tensile strength (Z) rests on 1 product, notched Charpy impact on 2 products and price is not published. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against PA6/66, PEI / ULTEM is 8 % weaker flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 79 °C higher. Against PC, PEI / ULTEM is 13 % tougher in notched impact and 7 % weaker flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 90 °C higher. Among the materials, PEI / ULTEM has a high tensile strength (Z) and HDT at 0.45 MPa, and a low elongation at break (XY): each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PEI / ULTEM typically reaches a tensile strength of 56 MPa at a modulus of 2.25 GPa, and breaks at 3 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 104 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.23 GPa.

Thermal properties

PEI / ULTEM's HDT is typically 208 °C under 0.45 MPa and 175⁠–⁠215 °C under 1.8 MPa, with its glass transition typically at 198 °C. As published, Vicat softening is typically 173 °C.

What makers say about printing it

Moisture sensitivity

  • During processing the moisture level should not exceed 0.03%, otherwise porosity and surface defects (e.g. smearing) may occur. LEHVOSS LUVOCOM 3F PEI 50236 GY, data sheet, p. 2

Printing advice

  • Keep the 3D printer out of the sun. 3D4Makers PEI Filament Ultem 1010, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
More from makers: nozzle wear, adhesion between layers, warping and shrinkage, supports and removal and odour and emissions

Nozzle wear

  • nozzle material: abrasion resistant LEHVOSS LUVOCOM 3F PEI 50236 GY, data sheet, p. 2

Adhesion between layers

  • The material combine excellent layer bonding, dimensional accuracy, a high impact resistance, durablitity and a high tensile strength into one filament. FormFutura PEI ULTEM 9085, data sheet, p. 1

Warping and shrinkage

  • It combines exceptional dimensional stability with good chemical resistance. FormFutura PEI ULTEM 9085, data sheet, p. 1

Supports and removal

  • ULTEM™ 1010 resin is used with breakaway support material Stratasys ULTEM, data sheet, p. 2

Odour and emissions

  • This cannot be a room where people sleep. 3D4Makers PEI Filament Ultem 1010, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • PEI 9085 has a translucent colour of amber and it is widely used in application such as medical, electrical/electronic, automotive, and aerospace industries. iSANMATE PEI 9085, data sheet, p. 1
  • Its high heat resistance makes it autoclave-capable for applications involving sterilization and composite lay-up tooling. Stratasys ULTEM, data sheet, p. 2
  • Nanovia PEI is resistant to autoclave operation, sterilisation cycles, and adapted for tooling able to resist high temperatures. Nanovia PEI Ultem 1010, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • High dimensionally stable precision parts. LEHVOSS LUVOCOM 3F PEI 50236 GY, data sheet, p. 1

Chemicals and environment

No maker of these products publishes resistance to a named chemical, to water or to sunlight.

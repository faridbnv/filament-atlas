TPU-LW is a foaming thermoplastic polyurethane, a lightweight flexible elastomer. Makers sell it for footwear, weight-saving drone and robot parts, and flexible wearables. Printed flat, it typically reaches a tensile strength of 10.3 MPa and breaks at 629 % elongation. On the H2C every product calls for drying, and makers warn of stringing and oozing as the foam expands in the hotend.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Footwear Innovation: Complete shoes, insoles, and soles Recreus FILAFLEX FOAMY, data sheet, p. 1
  • Lightweighting: Significantly reducing weight for drone parts, robotics, and more. Siraya Tech Flex TPU Air, data sheet, p. 1
  • As a flexible foaming material, the printed model is soft and skin-friendly, suitable for printing as flexible wearables and COSPLAY models. eSUN TPU LW, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • Due to the foaming nature of this material, stringing will be more pronounced than with standard TPU. Recreus FILAFLEX FOAMY, data sheet, p. 2
  • Active foaming materials naturally expand in the hotend, causing oozing during travel moves. Siraya Tech Flex TPU Air, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • Imagine creating parts up to 50% lighter than standard TPU, with a unique, soft, fabric-like texture straight off the print bed! Siraya Tech Flex TPU Air, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Soft, Skin-Friendly Texture: Smooth, comfortable finish Recreus FILAFLEX FOAMY, data sheet, p. 1

PP is polypropylene. Makers sell it for lightweight and chemically exposed parts such as drones and toys, and a maker warns it turns brittle at sub-zero temperatures. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 59 °C. On the H2C it prints at 225⁠–⁠230 °C with no heated chamber, but Prusa Research's knowledge base says unfilled polypropylene adheres poorly to the bed and warps.

Partly documented Tensile strength (XY) and HDT at 0.45 MPa rest on 1 product each, price on 2 products, tensile strength (Z) is not published and tensile modulus (XY) and notched Charpy impact are published only on test bars no document describes as printed. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar. Products with an added filler or additive are shown but kept out of the medians.

Among the materials

Against PLA, PP is 2.3 times as expensive. Against PETG, PP is 3.0 times as expensive. Among the materials, PP has a high elongation at break (XY) and a low tensile strength (XY), HDT at 0.45 MPa and density: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Its price sits in the middle half.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Model aircraft ,Decorative items, toys, accessories, figurines, and lighting fixtures. SUNLU PP, data sheet, p. 1
  • Pegasus PP combines all the mechanical properties of PP with an extremely lightweight and by that makes it a very suitable material for applications such as automotive, drones, floating applications, wear resistant & durable applications, etc… FormFutura Pegasus PP, data sheet, p. 1
  • Cultural and creative, Toys, Gifts and Industrial. iSANMATE PP, data sheet, p. 1
  • Low-density parts and chemically exposed prototypes; verify exact exposure. after Prusa Research, Polypropylene (PP) | Prusa Knowledge Base

What makers warn about

  • Please note the poor cold properties, which make PP brittle at sub-zero temperatures. Fabru Polypropylene (PP), data sheet, p. 1
  • Fibre filling may reduce warping, but does not establish universal strength or fatigue improvement. after Prusa Research, Polypropylene (PP) | Prusa Knowledge Base

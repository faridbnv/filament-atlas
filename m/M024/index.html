PETG-CF is a carbon-fibre-filled PETG copolyester. Makers sell it for end-use parts that do not need the full strength of carbon, and for structural parts and fixtures. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 72 °C, near its glass transition, and a maker notes the added stiffness costs impact resistance. On the H2C every product needs a hardened nozzle, and most print at 240⁠–⁠260 °C without chamber heat.

Among the materials

Carbon fibre adds little to PETG's heat resistance: PETG-CF's HDT at 0.45 MPa is 72 °C against 70 °C. Against PETG-GF, PETG-CF is 58 % stiffer, 10 % stronger flat and 7 % stronger across the layers. Among the materials, PETG-CF has a high tensile modulus (XY) and density, and a low HDT at 0.45 MPa and price: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PETG-CF typically reaches a tensile strength of 49.4 MPa at a modulus of 3.7 GPa, and breaks at 5.7 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 80 MPa at a flexural modulus of 3.21 GPa.

Thermal properties

PETG-CF's HDT is typically 72 °C under 0.45 MPa and 68 °C under 1.8 MPa, with its glass transition typically at 69 °C. As published, Vicat softening is typically 82 °C.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • PETG end use parts that do not require the full strength of carbon. FormFutura CarbonFil CF03, data sheet, p. 1
  • Structural parts, decorative items, toys, accessories, and figurines. SUNLU PETG Carbon Fiber, data sheet, p. 1
  • This makes it suitable for a variety of applications, including printing flower pots, fixtures, and water valve switches. Eryone PETG-CF, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • No. Carbon fiber reinforced filaments, including XPETG CF, are not certified for food contact. Any application requiring food safety should use a non-reinforced filament with confirmed food-contact compliance, such as PETG. Extrudr XPETG CF, maker's product page or guide, p.1
  • For an outdoor usage, it’s recommended either paint or apply a protective UV coating. Nanovia PETG CF, data sheet, p. 1

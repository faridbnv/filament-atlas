PET is polyethylene terephthalate. Makers sell it for protective parts, bumpers and covers and for large objects with fine detail, and they stress its low tendency to warp. Its glass transition is 70 °C. On the H2C it prints at 240⁠–⁠250 °C, and some sheets say no heated chamber is needed.

Among the materials

Against PET-CF, PET is 61 % less stiff and 8 % weaker flat. Against PET-GF, PET is 46 % less stiff. Among the materials, PET has a high density and a low notched Charpy impact: both are in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish them. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PET typically reaches a tensile strength of 58.4 MPa at a modulus of 2.25 GPa, and breaks at 7.8 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 91.1 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.3 GPa.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Thanks to minimal warping, easy printability and a wide processing window of 200–300°C , PET FLEX MAX is ideal for demanding functional parts exposed to bending, impact and repeated deformation. colorFabb PET Flex Max, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • This makes EasyFil PET ideal for 3D printing big objects with fine detail. FormFutura EasyFil PET, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • For applications requiring greater rigidity, a more structurally rigid filament may be preferable. colorFabb PET Flex Max, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • The material is not intended to behave like a very soft or highly flexible filament. colorFabb PET HIGH SPEED PRO, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • In addition to its mechanical and processing benefits, PET HIGH SPEED PRO produces an attractive high-gloss finish . colorFabb PET HIGH SPEED PRO, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • high surface quality after printing Spectrum ecoPET 9021, data sheet, p. 1
  • This unique set of properties translates to a PET filament that enables you to 3D print accurate parts with improved strength and mechanical properties. FormFutura EasyFil PET, data sheet, p. 1
  • Its excellent processing behaviour enables high printing speeds while maintaining consistent extrusion, dimensional accuracy and a high-quality surface finish. colorFabb PET HIGH SPEED PRO, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

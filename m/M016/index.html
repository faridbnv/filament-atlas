PLA Glow is glow-in-the-dark polylactic acid. Makers sell it for toys, signage, nameplates, logos and jewellery. It is amorphous as printed, so it softens at its glass transition: its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 55 °C. On the H2C it needs no heated chamber, but some makers call for a hardened steel or ruby nozzle because it wears the nozzle.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PLA Glow typically reaches a tensile strength of 37.6 MPa at a modulus of 2.01 GPa, and breaks at 5.15 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 74.2 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.81 GPa.

Thermal properties

PLA Glow is amorphous as printed, so it softens at its glass transition, typically 59 °C: its HDT is 55 °C under 0.45 MPa and 52 °C under 1.8 MPa. As published, Vicat softening is typically 55 °C and melting typically 159 °C.

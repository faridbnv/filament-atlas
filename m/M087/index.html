POM / Acetal is polyoxymethylene, also called acetal. Makers sell it for functional technical parts, snap connections and gears. It is semicrystalline. On the H2C most products print at 220⁠–⁠240 °C, but some ask for more bed heat than the H2C gives, and a maker warns that formaldehyde is released if the processing temperature is too high.

Among the materials

Against PLA, POM / Acetal is 26 % stronger flat. Against OBC, POM / Acetal is 4.8 times as strong flat. Among the materials, POM / Acetal has a high elongation at break (XY) and density: both are in the top quarter of the materials that publish them. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), POM / Acetal typically reaches a tensile strength of 57.5 MPa at a modulus of 1.87 GPa, and breaks at 23 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 84 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.45 GPa.

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • Tarfuse® POM is designed for printing functional technical parts, due to the material features dedicated to printing parts up to 100mm. Grupa Azoty Tarfuse POM 3D Filament, data sheet, p. 1
  • POM filament is therefore one of the preferred construction materials, e.g. B. for snap connections and gears. Fabru Polyoxymethylen (POM), data sheet, p. 1
  • optimal for snap connections Fabru Polyoxymethylen (POM), data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • They are not resistant to concentrated acids and hydrofluoric acid as well as oxidizing agents. Fabru Polyoxymethylen (POM), data sheet, p. 1
  • The weather resistance is not good. Fabru Polyoxymethylen (POM), data sheet, p. 1

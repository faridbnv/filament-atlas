ABS-AF is aramid-fibre-filled ABS. Makers sell it for lighter, fibre-reinforced parts that are easier to print, and stress its dimensional stability and low warping. Its glass transition is 110 °C. On the H2C it prints at 250⁠–⁠270 °C, and a maker recommends a ruby or hardened nozzle while another calls the fibre less abrasive than carbon or glass.

Among the materials

Aramid fibre lowers ABS's HDT at 0.45 MPa by 7 °C: ABS-AF's HDT at 0.45 MPa is 88 °C against 95 °C. Against ABS-CF, ABS-AF's HDT at 0.45 MPa is about the same: 88 °C against 90 °C. Among the materials, ABS-AF has a high notched Charpy impact and a low tensile strength (XY) and density: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Moisture sensitivity

  • Dry box not necessary Spectrum ABS Kevlar, data sheet, p. 1
More from makers: warping and shrinkage, adhesion between layers and odour and emissions

Warping and shrinkage

  • Low warping when printing (even less than with carbon fiber composites) Nanovia ABS AF, data sheet, p. 1
  • The applied modifications led to reduced shrinkage, excellent adhesion to work platforms and reduced material density, which means that the printed elements are slightly lighter than their counterparts made with standard ABS. Spectrum ABS Kevlar, data sheet, p. 1
  • Thanks to the aramid fibers Nanovia ABS AF allows for excellent 3D printing properties with limited warping and a great bed adhesion. Nanovia ABS AF, data sheet, p. 1
  • The aramid fibers assuring dimensional stability ,preventing the printed object to warp and even make the printed object lighter than regular ABS . 3D4Makers ABSKevlar, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • No shrinkage / dimensional stability. 3D4Makers ABSKevlar, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Low Warping. 3D4Makers ABSKevlar, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • excellent dimensional stability Spectrum ABS Kevlar, maker's product page or guide, p. 21

Adhesion between layers

  • very strong layer lamination Spectrum ABS Kevlar, data sheet, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • 10% Aramid fiber reinforced, high rigidity & impact strength, excellent dimensional stability Spectrum ABS Kevlar, maker's product page or guide, p. 21
  • Create aramid fibre reinforced 3D prints that are lighter, more resistant, and easier to print compared to native ABS, thank to Nanovia ABS AF. Nanovia ABS AF, data sheet, p. 1
  • ABS KEVLAR has been designed for 3D printing by a precise formulation of aramid fibers into ABS materials. 3D4Makers ABSKevlar, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • For an outdoor usage, we recommend that you paint your print or submit them to an anti UV treatment, such as our Nanovia smoothing solution. Nanovia ABS AF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Aramdid fibers and ABS are sensitive to UV radiation. Nanovia ABS AF, data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • Can be smoothed with acetone Nanovia ABS AF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Smooth surface. 3D4Makers ABSKevlar, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

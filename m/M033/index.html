ASA-CF is carbon-fibre-filled acrylonitrile styrene acrylate. Makers sell it for durable large models and outdoor parts, citing UV stability. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 101 °C, and across the layers it keeps 56 % of its flat stiffness. On the H2C it needs a hardened nozzle, and makers advise drying it before printing.

Partly documented Notched Charpy impact rests on 3 products and tensile strength (Z) on 4 products. Among the materials

Carbon fibre raises ASA's HDT at 0.45 MPa by 6 °C: ASA-CF's HDT at 0.45 MPa is 101 °C against 96 °C. Against ASA-GF, ASA-CF is 69 % stiffer and 12 % stronger across the layers, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 8 °C higher. Among the materials, ASA-CF has a high tensile modulus (XY) and a low density: both are in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish them. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), ASA-CF typically reaches a tensile strength of 39.1 MPa at a modulus of 3.91 GPa, and breaks at 6.3 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 78 MPa at a flexural modulus of 3.74 GPa.

More from makers: moisture sensitivity, supports and removal, warping and shrinkage, adhesion between layers and odour and emissions

Moisture sensitivity

  • As the ASACF10 filament absorbs moisture easily due to the added carbon fiber, it should be dried before being used. Flashforge ASACF10, data sheet, p. 3

Supports and removal

  • High heatbed temperatures and glue stick can cause strong adhesion between the print and the build plate. Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • It also has heat resistance close to 100°C, which is the first choice of outdoor reinforcement material. Polymaker FIBERON ASA CF08, data sheet, p. 1
  • Composite reinforced with 12% carbon fibre for high-strength applications 3DJake ASA CF, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • The material is reinforced with carbon fibres, making it ideal for mechanically highly stressed components. Extrudr DURAPRO ASA CF, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • Fiberon™ ASA-CF08 contains 8% carbon fiber as reinforcement, the filament itself is easy to be brittle, please make sure that the bending diameter is more than 60mm when using it. Polymaker FIBERON ASA CF08, data sheet, p. 1
  • Yes. Like all ASA filaments, it releases VOCs such as styrene when heated. Always print in a well-ventilated space, and ideally use an enclosure with a filtration system. Polymaker FIBERON ASA CF08, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • when the printing speed is too high, the melt flow state will become unstable, and the filaments extruded from the nozzle will no longer have the skin-core structure anymore, which can cause the rough surface of the printed part. QIDI ASA-CF20 Core, data sheet, p. 5
  • All of this, combined with a carbon matt surface that effectively masks the layers. Spectrum ASA-X CF10, data sheet, p. 1
  • A state-of-the-art manufacturing process achieves very low manufacturing tolerances, which leads to impressive printing results for all FDM 3D printers. Fabru ASA CF10, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • low diameter and roundness tolerances Fabru ASA CF10, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

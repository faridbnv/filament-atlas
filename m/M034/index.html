ASA-GF is a glass-fibre-filled acrylonitrile styrene acrylate. Makers sell it for end-use parts such as garden furniture and automotive interiors, and they report excellent resistance to outdoor exposure. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 88⁠–⁠98 °C, below its glass transition, and across the layers it keeps 67 % of its flat tensile strength. On the H2C most products need a hardened nozzle.

Among the materials

Glass fibre adds little to ASA's heat resistance: ASA-GF's HDT at 0.45 MPa is 93 °C against 96 °C. Against ASA-CF, ASA-GF is 41 % less stiff and 11 % weaker across the layers, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 8 °C lower. Among the materials, ASA-GF has a low density: it is in the lowest quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), ASA-GF typically reaches a tensile strength of 39 MPa at a modulus of 2.31 GPa, and breaks at 5.55 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 72.5 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.76 GPa.

Printing advice

  • After the printing process, it is recommended to dry the model in the oven at 80°C for 1-3 hours to increase the strength of the model. Flashforge ASAGF10, data sheet, p. 3

Moisture sensitivity

  • As the ASAGF10 filament absorbs moisture easily due to the added glass fiber, it should be dried before being used. Flashforge ASAGF10, data sheet, p. 3

Adhesion between layers

  • DuraPro ASA GF is optimized for good adhesion layer, thermal stability, improved flow properties, and low warping. Extrudr DURAPRO ASA GF, data sheet, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • It is used in outdoor and outdoor plastic products in direct contact with sunlight. iSANMATE ASA Glass Fiber, data sheet, p. 1
  • The material is reinforced with glass fibers, making it ideal for mechanically stressed components. Extrudr DURAPRO ASA GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • This material is ideal for applications requiring specific strength or weather resistance in end-use products, such as automotive interior components, garden furniture, and outdoor leisure structures. Eryone ASA - GF, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • No. Glass fiber reinforced filaments, including DuraPro ASA GF, are not certified for food contact. Extrudr DURAPRO ASA GF, maker's product page or guide, p.1
Finish and accuracy
  • The printed parts are robust and feature a matte, frosted surface texture. Eryone ASA - GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Diverse Colors : Black, white, grey; blends function with aesthetics. iSANMATE ASA Glass Fiber, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

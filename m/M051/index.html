PA6-GF is glass-fibre-reinforced polyamide 6. Makers sell it for gear wheels, bearings and sliding elements, and for demanding industrial and automotive uses. It is semicrystalline, so it carries light load well above its glass transition: its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 186 °C. On the H2C it needs a hardened nozzle, and makers stress dry storage because it is sensitive to moisture.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY) rests on 1 product, notched Charpy impact on 2 products and tensile strength (XY), tensile strength (Z) and price on 3 products each. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PA6-GF typically reaches a tensile strength of 89 MPa at a modulus of 4.26 GPa, and breaks at 6.08 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 124 MPa at a flexural modulus of 4.87 GPa.

What makers say about printing it

Moisture sensitivity

  • PolyMide™ PA6-GF is sensitive to moisture and should always be stored and used under dry conditions (relative humidity below 20%). Polymaker PolyMide PA6 GF, data sheet, p. 5

Nozzle wear

  • Normally, the life of a brass nozzle would be approximately 9h. Polymaker PolyMide PA6 GF, data sheet, p. 5

TPU harder than 95A is a harder thermoplastic polyurethane elastomer, rated at the hard end of Shore A or on the Shore D scale. Makers sell it for prototypes, end-use parts and rugged industrial applications. Its tensile strength (XY) is 30⁠–⁠53.7 MPa, and across the layers it keeps 64 % of its flat strength. On the H2C most products call for drying, and a maker says to dry it thoroughly before every print.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (Z), notched Charpy impact and price rest on 1 product each. Each figure is the median of the products that publish it comparably, and beside it the middle half of them; a value on a test bar no document describes as printed, or with no stated test load, is set apart, except a heat deflection of a plastic that cannot crystallise, which its glass transition sets, unless its document points to a moulded or resin bar.

Among the materials

Against TPU 95A class, TPU harder than 95A is 26 % stronger flat. Against TPC / TPEE, TPU harder than 95A is 3.1 times as strong flat. Among the materials, TPU harder than 95A has a high elongation at break (XY) and notched Charpy impact, and a low tensile modulus (XY) and price: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. What makers say about printing it

Printing advice

  • After the printing process, it is recommended to dry the model in the oven at 120-130°C for 6-8 hours to increase the strength of the model. Flashforge TPU 64D, data sheet, p. 3

Moisture sensitivity

  • Longer drying times may be needed if the filament has been exposed to humid air for extended periods. Siraya Tech Flex TPU 64D, data sheet, p. 1

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • It offers temperature resistance up to 100 °C and a high level of chemical resistance making TPU 98A suitable for rugged industrial applications. BigRep TPU 98A, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Save time and money using Armadillo to prove your concepts. Print your product in a fraction of the time it would take with traditional methods. NinjaTek Armadillo, maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • This property gives the prints an elegant and high-quality appearance that is ideal for aesthetically demanding applications. Extrudr FLEX MEDIUM MATT, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • Only printable with Direct Drive Extruder - low printing speed Fabru Thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU), data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • Unlike other flexible filaments Nanovia TPU 70 is rigid enough to be sanded. Nanovia TPU 70D, data sheet, p. 1
  • Briefly applying heat (e.g., heat gun on low setting, very quick pass with a torch flame) can sometimes smooth surfaces or reduce fuzziness, but exercise extreme caution to avoid melting or burning the part. Siraya Tech Flex TPU 64D, data sheet, p. 1
  • Using a state-of-the-art manufacturing process, very low manufacturing tolerances are achieved, which leads to convincing printing results with all FDM 3D printers. Fabru Thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU), maker's product page or guide, p. 1
  • Consistent diameter and material properties providing reliable, high-quality prints NinjaTek Armadillo, maker's product page or guide, p. 1

PA12-GF is glass-fibre-filled polyamide 12. A maker sells it for functional parts and for parts that absorb kinetic energy or repeated shock, and a maker stresses its very low moisture absorption. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 150 °C. On the H2C it prints at 255⁠–⁠270 °C and needs a hardened nozzle, and a sheet asks for more chamber heat than the H2C reaches.

Among the materials

Among the materials, PA12-GF has a high tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY) and price, and a low density: each is in the top or bottom quarter of the materials that publish it. Its elongation at break (XY) and HDT at 0.45 MPa sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PA12-GF typically reaches a tensile strength of 82 MPa at a modulus of 7.8 GPa, and breaks at 4.5 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 94 MPa at a flexural modulus of 7.55 GPa.

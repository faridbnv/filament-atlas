TPS is a styrenic thermoplastic elastomer. Makers sell it for seals, shoe materials and uses needing rubber-like elasticity, and its glass transition is -59 °C. On the H2C it prints at 225⁠–⁠245 °C, but a maker says it prints only with a direct-drive extruder and at low speed, and another advises drying before printing.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (XY), tensile strength (Z) and price rest on 1 product each and notched Charpy impact is not published. What makers say about printing it

Printing advice

  • Use brim for better bed adhesion. Fillamentum Flexfill TPE 90A, data sheet, p. 1
  • It is recommended to use a short-range double-wheel deceleration extruder designed for flexibility, and eLastic materials cannot be printed by a remote extruder. eSUN TPE 83A, data sheet, p. 2
  • After a long printing time, impurities may remain in the nozzle, so it is recommended to use it with cleaning filament. eSUN TPE 83A, data sheet, p. 2
  • If necessary, pleasw replace the nozzle and throat with new ones. eSUN TPE 83A, data sheet, p. 2
  • Workability of 3D printing filament is at least 12 months from delivery. Fillamentum Flexfill TPE 96A, data sheet, p. 1
  • Try your own settings before printing. Fillamentum Flexfill TPE 96A, data sheet, p. 1
  • As with other flexible materials, the smaller the filling and the thinner the wall thickness, the more elas+c the printed component remains. Fabru Thermoplastic styrene block copolymer elastomer (TPS), data sheet, p. 1

Moisture sensitivity

  • it is recommended to dry the filaments (55℃/> 4h) before printing to achieve the best printing effect, and it would be better to use together with eBOX when printing. eSUN TPE 83A, data sheet, p. 2

Adhesion between layers

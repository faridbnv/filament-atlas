ABS-GF is ABS filled with glass fibre. Makers sell it for mechanical parts, and a maker adds tooling fixtures, jigs and enclosures. Its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 98 °C and its glass transition 104 °C, and a maker notes that the glass fibre lowers elongation at break. On the H2C every product needs a hardened nozzle, and makers call for an enclosure to limit warping.

Among the materials

Glass fibre adds little to ABS's heat resistance: ABS-GF's HDT at 0.45 MPa is 98 °C against 95 °C. Against ABS-CF, ABS-GF is 37 % less stiff and 6 % stronger flat, and its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 8 °C higher. Among the materials, ABS-GF has a low elongation at break (XY), density and price: each is in the lowest quarter of the materials that publish it. The rest of its key properties sit in the middle half.

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), ABS-GF typically reaches a tensile strength of 46.5 MPa at a modulus of 2.75 GPa, and breaks at 2.1 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 70 MPa at a flexural modulus of 3.3 GPa.

After printing

  • the suggested annealing temperature of models printed with Bambu ABS-GF is 80 to 90 °C, and the time is 6 to 12 hours. Bambu Lab ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 3

What makers say about printing it

Warping and shrinkage

  • An enclosure is crucial for maintaining consistent printing temperatures, ideally around 50°C, to significantly reduce issues like warping and failure rates, particularly in cold climates. Siraya Tech Fibreheart ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 3

Printing advice

  • While nozzles larger than 0.2mm work, a 0.4mm nozzle often yields the best results, ensuring long-term printing consistency and quality. Siraya Tech Fibreheart ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 3
More from makers: nozzle wear, moisture sensitivity, odour and emissions, adhesion between layers and supports and removal

Nozzle wear

  • For printing Fibreheart ABS-GF, use a hardened steel or diamond-tipped nozzle to withstand the abrasive nature of glass fiber. Siraya Tech Fibreheart ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 3

Moisture sensitivity

  • Using a dry box with desiccant, maintaining humidity below 15% is ideal for prolonged storage. Siraya Tech Fibreheart ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 4

Odour and emissions

  • Thanks to this advanced production process, the residual amount of solvents and monomers used in the production process in the final ABS product is so low that the filament has a low odor during printing. QIDI NexABS-GF25, data sheet, p. 2

Adhesion between layers

  • It has fixed the defect that the ordinary fiber-reinforced material will lose the bonding strength between layers. QIDI NexABS-GF25, data sheet, p. 3

Supports and removal

  • QIDI S-White Quick-Remove Support Material QIDI NexABS-GF25, data sheet, p. 5

Uses, limitations and chemicals

What it is sold for

  • ABS-GF is ideal for both mechanical parts and decorations due to the inherited water resistance, wear resistance, durability and vibrant colors. Bambu Lab ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Engineering/functional parts, ornaments, toys, accessories, figurines. SUNLU ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • Aerospace • Automotive • Industrial applications eSUN eABS-GF, data sheet, p. 1

What makers warn about

  • The disadvantage of glass fiber reinforcement is the lower elongation at break. Fabru Acrylnitrilbutadienstyrol glass fiber 10% (ABS GF10), data sheet, p. 1
Finish and accuracy
  • when the printing speed is too high, the melt flow state will become unstable, and the filaments extruded from the nozzle will no longer have the skin-core structure anymore, which can cause the rough surface of the printed part. QIDI NexABS-GF25, data sheet, p. 6
  • Glass fiber imparts a smooth matte finish with micro-spot gloss, blurring layer line. Bambu Lab ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 1
  • fine detail reproduction SUNLU ABS-GF, data sheet, p. 1

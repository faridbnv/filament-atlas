PLA Sparkle is polylactic acid with glitter or metal particles. Makers sell it for toys and for prints with a sparkling look. It is amorphous as printed, so it softens at its glass transition: its HDT at 0.45 MPa is 51⁠–⁠55 °C. On the H2C it prints at 200⁠–⁠230 °C, and a maker advises against a very small nozzle because it may clog.

Partly documented Tensile modulus (XY), tensile strength (Z), notched Charpy impact and price rest on 1 product each and tensile strength (XY) and HDT at 0.45 MPa on 2 products each. Among the materials

Mechanical properties

Printed flat (XY), PLA Sparkle typically reaches a tensile strength of 49 MPa at a modulus of 1.94 GPa, and breaks at 12 % elongation. In bending, its flexural strength is typically 86 MPa at a flexural modulus of 2.73 GPa.

Thermal properties

PLA Sparkle is amorphous as printed, so it softens at its glass transition, typically 56 °C: its HDT is 50.8⁠–⁠55 °C under 0.45 MPa and 53 °C under 1.8 MPa. As published, Vicat softening is typically 56 °C and melting typically 160 °C.
